QuickFit Privacy Policy

Effective as of 2026-11-16

This privacy policy applies to the QuickFit app (the "Application") for mobile devices, created by Nguyen Chu (the "Service Provider") as a free service. The service is provided "AS IS".

Summary

QuickFit has no accounts and no ads. Your workout history stays on your device. The Application sends anonymous usage data to an analytics service so the Service Provider can see how the app is used and improve it.

Information that stays on your device

Your workout history, progress and streaks are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the Service Provider or to anyone else, and they are deleted when you uninstall the Application.

Information the Application collects

When you use the Application, it sends the following to the analytics service:

  • A random identifier created when the Application is installed. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, advertising ID or any account.
  • App activity: when the Application is opened or put in the background, which screens are shown, and when a workout is started, completed, rated or left early, including which exercise you were on.
  • Device and app information: device manufacturer and model, operating system and version, screen size, app version, language setting and time zone.
  • Your device's IP address, which is transmitted automatically with every request and may be used to estimate your country or city.

The Application does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, health data, or the precise location of your device. The Application never asks you to provide personal information.

How the information is used

The information is used only to understand how the Application is used, for example how many people finish a workout and where people stop, and to fix problems and improve the Application. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to identify you.

Third-party services

Usage data is processed on behalf of the Service Provider by PostHog, Inc. and stored on PostHog's servers in the United States. PostHog's privacy policy is available at https://posthog.com/privacy.

The Application is distributed through Google Play, which has its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The Service Provider may also disclose information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

Opt-out rights

You can stop all collection of information by uninstalling the Application, using the standard uninstall process on your device or in the app marketplace.

Data retention and deletion

Usage data is kept for as long as it is useful for improving the Application. Because the data is not linked to your identity, the Service Provider cannot normally tell which data came from your device. If you would like your data deleted, contact toeffen@gmail.com and the Service Provider will help as far as it is possible to identify the data.

Children

The Application is not directed at anyone under the age of 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider at toeffen@gmail.com so the necessary actions can be taken.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Usage data is transmitted over an encrypted connection, and access to it is limited to the Service Provider.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes by updating this page. You are advised to consult this privacy policy regularly, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

Your consent

By using the Application, you consent to the processing of your information as set out in this privacy policy, now and as amended.

Contact us

If you have any questions about privacy while using the Application, please contact the Service Provider by email at toeffen@gmail.com.
